# NKP4 Homepage Architecture

This is the recommended narrative order, not a fixed wireframe.

## 1. Navigation

Keep it minimal.

Potential first-version navigation:

- Companies
- Story
- Contact

or

- Portfolio
- About
- Contact

Avoid deep dropdowns while the portfolio is small.

## 2. Hero

The hero should establish NKP4 quickly.

Working direction:

# Build. Operate. Equip. Invest.

Supporting thought:

> NKP4 is the holding company and business home for companies, technology, investments, and ideas built or backed by Nielsen Aragon.

Alternative conceptual lead:

> **Turning experience into leverage.**

Do not publish either line automatically. Test them against the visual design and surrounding copy.

Primary action:

**Explore the portfolio ↓**

The hero should be visually confident without relying on animation.

## 3. Portfolio / Companies

This should appear early.

The portfolio is evidence.

First known entries:

### NKP4 Technology

**Operating Company · AI & Technology Consulting**

A way for businesses to access Nielsen's technology and operating experience directly.

### SureHelp Inc.

**Founder & Operator · Insurtech**

Insurance technology built around the workflows and routines that help agents run stronger books of business.

### Manifest Network

**Investment · Web3**

Outside investment in a Web3 company focused on Proof of Authority.

Use exact portfolio descriptions from `05-PORTFOLIO.md` when publishing.

## 4. What NKP4 does

A concise four-part framework:

### Build
Create companies, products, software, and new ventures.

### Operate
Help businesses build stronger systems and execute consistently.

### Equip
Give founders and operators access to modern technology, operating knowledge, and resources.

### Invest
Participate in opportunities where NKP4 can contribute more than capital.

## 5. Founder arc

Do not create a résumé timeline.

Possible visual/narrative progression:

**Bay Area → Technology → Operator → Founder → NKP4**

Use selected company logos as evidence rather than as trophies.

Potential source line:

> I sit, observe, immerse myself, then build the thing that gives people their time back.

## 6. Operating philosophy

Potential centerpiece:

# Accountability → Predictability → Profitability → Scalability

Provide only enough explanation to make the formula understandable.

A second philosophical layer may introduce:

> **Don't just sell the apple. Sell the seeds.**

Possible supporting thought:

> The companies are the output. The systems, experience, technology, and operating knowledge underneath them are what can compound.

## 7. What Nielsen is working on now

Optional section.

Keep it manually curated rather than turning it into a blog feed.

Potential categories:

- Building
- Operating
- Exploring

## 8. Contact / Networking

This is a primary feature, not footer housekeeping.

Possible heading:

# Let's connect.

The goal is to make NKP4 the single URL Nielsen can share when networking.

Potential destinations:

- Email
- LinkedIn
- direct contact form
- booking link, if desired
- NKP4 Technology
- SureHelp

Do not overload the section with every social network.

## 9. Footer

Simple.

```text
NKP4

Portfolio
About
Contact

LinkedIn
Email

© NKP4
```

## Mobile requirement

Mobile is especially important because the URL may be opened immediately after an in-person interaction.

Within the first few screens, someone should understand:

1. NKP4
2. Nielsen
3. the main companies
4. the core idea
5. how to reach him

# NKP4 Design References

These sites are references for patterns, not templates to clone.

Do not copy proprietary wording, page structure, graphics, or distinctive visual execution.

## 1. Mark Cuban Companies — PRIMARY REFERENCE

https://markcubancompanies.com/

### Why it matters

MCC is the closest conceptual reference for NKP4 because the website functions as a central home for a person, companies, investments, and business activity rather than behaving like a traditional investment fund.

### Study

- portfolio-first presentation
- founder + portfolio relationship
- company-directory model
- logo-driven browsing
- simple company descriptions
- breadth without excessive corporate explanation
- central destination for many different business relationships

### NKP4 adaptation

Use the same basic idea:

**one umbrella → many relationships → clear founder connection**

But improve the experience with:

- more modern typography
- stronger relationship labels
- clearer separation between operating companies and investments
- more editorial storytelling
- better mobile presentation
- stronger contact / networking function

### Do not copy

- MCC branding
- exact card layouts
- exact filters
- wording
- visual identity

---

## 2. Tiny

https://tiny.com/

### Use for

- plainspoken voice
- founder/operator personality
- clear philosophy
- simple company descriptions
- explaining why the holding company exists
- storytelling without sounding institutional

### NKP4 adaptation

Borrow the confidence and clarity, not Tiny's acquisition-company positioning.

NKP4 is not primarily a buyer of mature businesses.

---

## 3. Exor

https://www.exor.com/

### Use for

- restraint
- typography
- whitespace
- premium holding-company feel
- long-term perspective
- allowing portfolio companies to remain visually distinct

### NKP4 adaptation

Use Exor as an aesthetic reference for maturity, but avoid making an early founder-led holding company look like a public multinational corporation.

# Reference weighting

When making decisions, roughly weight references as:

- **55% MCC** — overall structural idea
- **20% Tiny** — voice and founder-led storytelling
- **15% Exor** — visual restraint and premium presentation
- **5% PSL** — portfolio ecosystem
- **5% Red Sea Ventures** — navigation/contact simplicity

This is directional, not mathematical.

# Overall NKP4 visual principle

The site should feel like:

**MCC's business hub + Tiny's human voice + Exor's restraint**

without resembling a clone of any of them.

# Visual anti-patterns

Avoid:

- generic AI gradients
- neon circuitry imagery
- floating glass cards everywhere
- stock photos of people shaking hands
- fake office imagery
- meaningless animated graphs
- excessive scroll effects
- “venture capital” visual clichés
- attempts to make NKP4 look larger than it is

Use instead:

- typography
- whitespace
- real logos
- real photography
- strong hierarchy
- simple grids
- subtle motion
- thoughtful editorial layouts

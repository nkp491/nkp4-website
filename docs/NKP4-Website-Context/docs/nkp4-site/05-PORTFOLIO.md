## SureHelp Inc.

**Category:** Operating / Portfolio Company  
**Industry:** Insurtech  
**Relationship:** Founder & Operator  
**Status:** Active

### What it is

SureHelp Inc. is an insurance technology company founded by Nielsen Aragon and currently one of his primary operating focuses.

SureHelp grew from observing how life insurance agents worked and identifying the mechanical work surrounding the human act of selling and servicing clients.

### Core philosophy

> **SureHelp handles everything about selling life insurance that isn't selling — so agents can build a book that stays.**

### Origin pattern

SureHelp reflects Nielsen's broader operating style:

1. observe the workflow
2. identify friction
3. understand what must remain human
4. build systems around the mechanical work
5. make good routines easier to repeat

### Known product / capability references from source material

- SurePortal
- SureConnect
- Capture
- Field Check
- Book of Business
- Agent Studio
- coverage certificates

Exact current product availability and public descriptions must be verified before publishing.

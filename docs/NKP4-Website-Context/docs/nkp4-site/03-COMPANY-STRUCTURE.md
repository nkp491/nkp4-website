# NKP4 Company Structure

## Parent organization

### NKP4

**Type:** Holding company  
**Founder:** Nielsen Aragon  
**Role:** Parent organization and umbrella for operating companies, services, ventures, investments, and future business activities.

NKP4 itself should not be presented as a standalone product or consulting service.

## Current known structure

```text
NKP4
│
├── NKP4 Technology
│   ├── Type: Operating company / service business
│   └── Focus: AI and technology consulting
│
├── SureHelp Inc.
│   ├── Type: Operating / portfolio company
│   ├── Focus: Insurtech
│   └── Relationship: Founded and actively operated/developed by Nielsen
│
└── Manifest Network
    ├── Type: Outside investment
    ├── Focus: Web3 / Proof of Authority
    └── Relationship: Investment
```

## Relationship taxonomy

Every portfolio item should include an explicit relationship label.

Supported labels may include:

- Founded
- Founder & Operator
- Operating Company
- NKP4 Company
- Investment
- Strategic Investment
- Partner
- Joint Venture
- Advisory
- Venture
- Project
- Incubating
- Exited

The exact relationship should always be factual.

## Important presentation rule

Do not display all companies in a way that implies NKP4 owns or controls all of them.

The website should clearly distinguish:

- businesses Nielsen founded
- businesses Nielsen actively operates
- services offered through NKP4 companies
- outside investments
- strategic relationships
- experimental projects
